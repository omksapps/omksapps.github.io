Photo Cleaner is published by OMK under the brand Lamm.

The short version

Your photographs never leave your phone. They are read, compared and deleted on the device. There is no account, no server of ours, and no picture is ever uploaded, copied out or backed up anywhere.

The app does use the internet, for exactly two things: showing one banner advertisement, and checking whether you have an active subscription. Neither of those involves your photographs.

What the app accesses on your device, and why

To find clutter the app needs to look at your gallery. It reads:

  • the metadata of your photos and videos — file size, date, dimensions, folder and file name — to identify screenshots, oversized videos and files old enough to be worth reviewing;
  • the contents of some files, to prove that two are identical or nearly so. This happens entirely in the phone's memory, and what is kept afterwards is a short fingerprint, stored in the app's own cache directory on your device;
  • optionally, a folder you grant access to — used only to find copies WhatsApp made of pictures already in your gallery, and its own status cache. You grant it explicitly and can revoke it in Android settings at any time.

None of this is transmitted anywhere.

Advertising

The app shows a single banner advertisement on its home screen, served by Google AdMob. To do that, AdMob receives information from your device — including your device's advertising ID, device and app information, and approximate location derived from your IP address — and may use it to select and measure ads. It does not receive your photographs, your gallery contents, or anything the app has found in them.

Google's handling of that data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form the first time it runs and respects your answer; you can change it later from the same screen. Subscribing removes the banner, and with it the ad requests.

Subscription

The subscription is sold and managed by Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; the app never sees your card details. The app asks Play whether your account owns an active subscription and stores only a yes/no answer on the device. You can manage or cancel at any time in Google Play.

What the app does not do

No analytics. No crash reporting. No user profiles. No sale of data. No tracking across other apps or websites. The app has no backend of its own at all.

Permissions

READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, and on Android 14 and later READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED for partial access. WAKE_LOCK for video playback. INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE for the advertisement and the subscription check. It deliberately does not request MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE.

Deletion

When you confirm a deletion, the app asks Android to delete those files. Android shows its own confirmation, and the app reports only what Android actually removed. On Android this deletion is permanent — the files do not go to a recycle bin — which is why the app asks twice, and why duplicate sets always keep one copy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
