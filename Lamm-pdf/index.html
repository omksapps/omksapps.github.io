Lamm PDF

Every PDF tool. One calm app.

Lamm PDF turns your phone into a complete PDF workshop. Scan paper into clean documents, sign a contract with your finger, shrink a file until it fits an email, merge and split, convert to and from images, and find a word inside a scan — in Arabic as well as English.

Every tool runs on your own device. Your documents are never uploaded, and there is no account to create. The app uses the network only to check whether you have subscribed, and to send anonymous diagnostics that never include a file name or anything your documents say.
